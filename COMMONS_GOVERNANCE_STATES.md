# Public Commons participation states

The initial public Commons uses the following lightweight evidence states for public work:

`PROPOSED -> REVIEWED -> REVISED -> ACCEPTED_FOR_PUBLIC_COMMONS`

These states are repository-level dispositions only. They do not automatically mean:

`VERIFIED`, `FOUNDER_APPROVED`, `QCORE_RATIFIED`, `CANONICAL`, `DEPLOYED`, `CURRENT`, `RUN_AUTHORIZED`, `MANAGE_DELEGATED`, `OWN_GRANTED`, or `CROWD_CROWNED`.

## Participation progression

The broader participation arc is directional rather than automatic:

`STRANGER -> LEARNER -> EXPLORER -> CONTRIBUTOR -> COLLABORATOR -> BUILDER -> STEWARD -> CO-GOVERNOR`

Progression should be evidence-based and challengeable. Activity volume, technical skill, funding, popularity or proximity alone do not create institutional authority.

## Launch-zero operating boundary

At launch zero, the Commons activates bounded SOURCE, BUILD and CROWD functions. Later stages require separately designed and evidenced identity, responsibility, challenge, delegation and readback mechanisms.
