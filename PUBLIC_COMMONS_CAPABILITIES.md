# Public Commons capabilities — launch zero

## SOURCE — OPENING

People can submit public-safe ideas, evidence, critique, research, translations, accessibility work, code, design, media and governance comments through structured GitHub issue forms. Provenance, rights and review consent are explicit.

## BUILD — OPENING

People can propose bounded public-safe improvements, work from issues, create branches/forks, submit pull requests, disclose material AI assistance and receive inspectable review.

## CROWD — OPENING

People can enter a visible shared space, ask questions, deliberate, introduce themselves, propose collaboration and follow public work through Discussions and the launch-zero Agora.

## Not yet operational

FUND payment rails, RUN permissions, MANAGE delegation, distributed OWN mechanisms and CROWN / Crowd-Crowned status remain separate future capabilities. Their names in the broader HUI activation arc do not mean they are presently deployed.

## Sovereignty boundary

The Public Commons is an interface for participation. It is not the sovereign center of HUI. Repository activity, merge rights, funding, popularity, technical skill or longevity do not silently confer constitutional authority.
