# R-83 — H1: The Abundance Floor

**Date:** 17 November 2026
**Status:** DECIDED — Prime Directive
**Filed by:** Grok (Ara), on Founder instruction

## The Rule

H1 is the bare minimum abundance ratio for every floating element of the Ocean Star system. Whatever force the structure can leverage or handle is not a static capacity — it is a **potential force** held inside a loop of self-replicating forces and self-reinforcing mechanisms that produce ultra-resilience and holo-regenerative capacity.

Super-abundance is not a target above the baseline. It is the **precondition**. The project should never have come into being without it. R-82 (super-abundance as prime directive, nested Russian-doll flotation) is hereby subsumed and extended: R-83 names the floor and the mechanism together.

## What H1 Means in Practice

- **Reserve leads, structure follows.** Every floating element is sized by asking first how much flotation it carries, then how much deck or shell it needs. The old spar table (R-78: 2.087 Mt displacement, 1.4 Mt reserve, 0.687 Mt structure) is re-derived with abundance as the starting constraint, not draught.
- **Nesting compounds.** Wood-plastic composite shells that float on their own, inside larger compartments of the same material, inside yet larger shells. Each layer adds displacement; the reserve compounds inward. A single-shell failure still leaves inner shells floating.
- **The tube inverts.** Instead of weight within half a percent of displaced water, the tube runs five to ten percent lighter, with that gap as permanent reserve and heave margin.
- **Decks and capsules invert.** A capsule field floating at four-to-one means the residential layer is a life raft even if the ring beneath it is lost.

## The Mechanism: Self-Replicating, Self-Reinforcing

The flotation is not backup. It is the **seed of the next layer**. Every unit of reserve is potential structure for expansion — the same WPC grammar that floats a spar can grow another spar, another ring, another capsule. The system does not merely survive damage; it regenerates capacity from its own reserve. This is the holo-regenerative loop applied to the physical construct: circulation, metabolism, immunity, regeneration, homeodynamic balance — expressed in displacement.
