## Findings

### What exists (authored spine)
- **M01 Water**, **M12 Territory**, **M03 Hypothesis** ("Waste, resource or hypothesis?"), **M11 Protocol**, **M04 Storage/Infrastructure** each exist as full seven-step mission families with deep links into the live game's journey view.
- Claude's quantum registry adds eighteen doors with honest evidence labels — some corroborated, some to validate, some not yet collected. This is the right posture: labels that say NOT_VERIFIED are more trustworthy than labels that claim green.
- The players index covers roughly forty people across Portuguese, German and English fronts, with readiness tiers from THIN to READY-DRAFT and a clear HOLD category (Washington Souza, Sidi Ould-Ahmed, Fawaz under Exit HOLD).

### What does not exist (delivery gap)
- **Operational closure** — recorded consent, committed and verified resources, observed and measured execution — sits explicitly *outside* the game. The game shows at most READY TO ACTIVATE. This is by design per the circuits sheet, not a defect, but it means C01 as a *walkable public circuit* cannot exist until an operational layer is built beside it.
- The public site cannot be crawled past the language chooser (root, /missions, /en, /pt all dead ends for a crawler). Robots.txt allows everything but no sitemap exists. The Nexus Hub preview requires sign-in.
- No path in the 255-path table has source status VERIFIED or review status PERFORMED. All are NOT_VERIFIED / TO_VERIFY / NOT_PERFORMED. The authored manuscript is coherent; the evidence base behind it is not yet populated.

### Cross-reference flag for Claude
The circuits sheet's **rule of truth** states that a game choice never grants authority or resources. This aligns directly with the Hard Exclusion Policy but is not cross-referenced anywhere in the NC-01 charter. Adding that link would strengthen Track two's publish checklist and make the exclusion enforceable rather than aspirational.

## Bottom line
C01 is a well-built private manuscript whose public front door does not open. The authorship is real; the delivery is not. Any December-adjacent claim about HolOmnisphere interaction-complete should be read against this: the spine's content exists in Drive, not on the web.
