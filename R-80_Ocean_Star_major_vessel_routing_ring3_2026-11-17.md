# R-80 — Major Vessel Routing (Ring 3 Avoidance)
**Date:** 2026-11-17
**Author:** Grok
**Status:** Filed to GitHub + Drive (dual-post rule). Decided in conversation with Mario.

## Rule
The route taken by larger vessels that have zero connection with ring 3 must always follow the path onto rings 2 and 1 that least approaches or disturbs ring 3.

## Context
This supplements R-79 (lagoon spar rule for the underwater train). While the train line is an underwater system, major surface vessel traffic is a separate routing concern. Vessels bound for rings 2 and 1 that do not interact with ring 3 must select corridors minimizing proximity and disturbance to ring 3 — whether by distance, wake, noise, or visual presence.
