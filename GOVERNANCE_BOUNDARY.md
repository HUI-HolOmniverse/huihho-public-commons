# Governance Boundary — HUI · H2O Public Commons

This public repository is a participation and co-creation surface. It is not the sovereign center of Humanity United International.

## Invariants

- contribution != truth
- contribution != authority
- contribution != ownership
- review != ratification
- merge != constitutional approval
- funding != sovereignty
- interoperability != sovereignty
- public popularity != decision authority

HUI's consequential governance remains subject to the applicable QCORE process, evidence, rights, affected stakeholders, constitutional invariants and explicit responsibility.

## What repository maintainers may do

Maintainers may triage, request changes, merge public-safe contributions, moderate scope/safety/rights issues, manage labels and maintain repository quality within delegated authority.

## What repository maintainers may not infer

Repository permissions do not by themselves confer authority over HUI as a whole, H2O legal governance, private repositories, capital allocation, regulated activity, protected IP, partner commitments, or future constitutional standing.

## Public commons decisions

A repository decision should be inspectable enough to show:
- the contribution/problem;
- relevant evidence;
- acceptance criteria;
- material rights/safety concerns;
- review disposition;
- resulting public change where applicable.

Disagreement and challenge are legitimate parts of the commons when conducted within the code of conduct.
