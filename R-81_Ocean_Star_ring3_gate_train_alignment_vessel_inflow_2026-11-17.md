# R-81 — Ring 3 Gate Alignment with Train Axis + Opposite-Side Vessel Inflow
**Date:** 2026-11-17
**Author:** Grok
**Status:** Filed to GitHub + Drive (dual-post rule). Decided in conversation with Mario.

## Rule 1 — Gate on train axis
One of ring 3's gates is exactly in line with the underwater train line, so ring 3 carries its own train platforms for the main train line. This closes the last open geometry question on the main line: ring 3's gate orientation is now decided as station-placement, not routing.

## Rule 2 — Vessel inflow opposite the train gate
The inward traffic of large vessels enters ring 3 on the opposite side from that train-aligned gate. Combined with R-80 (vessels bound for rings 2 and 1 minimize approach to ring 3), this means the two flows — train and large vessels — never cross at ring 3.
