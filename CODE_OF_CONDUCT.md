# Public Commons Code of Conduct

The HUI · H2O Public Commons is intended to support serious, humane, plural and co-creative participation.

## Expected conduct

Participants should:
- address ideas without degrading people;
- distinguish evidence, interpretation and preference;
- disclose material conflicts of interest where relevant;
- respect privacy, rights and attribution;
- avoid spam, harassment, threats, discrimination and manipulation;
- make room for disagreement and revision;
- avoid presenting personal views as ratified HUI positions;
- help newcomers understand the contribution process without gatekeeping by jargon.

## Moderation

Maintainers may edit labels, close or restrict threads, request changes, remove abusive material, or escalate sensitive matters when needed for safety, rights, scope or repository integrity.

Moderation decisions should be proportionate and, where practical, explain the governing reason.

## No capture through participation

High activity, funding, technical skill, social reach or closeness to maintainers does not automatically create authority over other contributors or over HUI/H2O.
