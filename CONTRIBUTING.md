# Contributing to the HUI · H2O Public Commons

Thank you for contributing to a public-safe part of Humanity United International / Humane Humanity Organization.

## What you can contribute

- ideas and critiques
- research and evidence
- translation and language work
- accessibility improvements
- documentation
- design
- code
- media/public-knowledge improvements
- governance comments

## Before submitting

Confirm that:
1. you have the right to share the material;
2. you are not posting confidential/private/security-sensitive content;
3. your contribution identifies relevant sources where possible;
4. you understand that contribution does not equal truth, authority, ownership, publication or canon;
5. you consent to public review of the contribution.

## Contribution states

A public contribution may move through states such as:

`PROPOSED -> REVIEWED -> REVISED -> ACCEPTED_FOR_PUBLIC_COMMONS`

These states do not automatically mean `VERIFIED`, `FOUNDER_APPROVED`, `QCORE_RATIFIED`, `CANONICAL`, `DEPLOYED`, or `CURRENT`.

## Build workflow

For code/design/documentation work:
1. choose or open a bounded issue;
2. state the problem and acceptance criteria;
3. fork/branch and make the smallest useful change;
4. test or inspect the result;
5. open a pull request linked to the issue;
6. disclose AI assistance when materially used;
7. respond to review;
8. preserve provenance and avoid unrelated scope expansion.

## Rights

By contributing, you represent that you own the contribution or have the right to submit/license it under the repository's applicable contribution terms. If material should only be referenced and not republished, clearly mark it as link-only/source-reference material.

## No automatic status

A contribution, donation, pull request, review, public visibility or sustained participation does not automatically create employment, partnership, equity, institutional authority, Holo-Co-Founder status, X-Membership, or sovereignty over HUI/H2O.

## Good first contributions

Initial public-safe work should favor:
- accessibility fixes;
- translation;
- documentation clarity;
- broken-link/readback fixes;
- public-safe research/source references;
- tests;
- small UI improvements;
- provenance improvements.
